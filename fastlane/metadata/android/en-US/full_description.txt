Mohakash brings the Astronomy Picture of the Day to your phone.

Read today's picture and the full explanation, browse the complete archive, keep favourites for offline reading, and put astronomy pictures on your home screen with a genuine Android live wallpaper.

Features:
- Today's APOD with the date, credit, title and explanation.
- Searchable archive with year, media and date filters.
- Favourites, offline-friendly metadata and a private History of random discoveries and carousel wallpaper choices.
- One-picture-a-day wallpaper or a carousel that rotates through valid APODs from the same date in past years, with a next-wallpaper countdown and a Skip action.
- A real WallpaperService with Fill or Fit, optional Wi-Fi-only downloads and optional notifications for new APOD pictures.
- Hide videos in the feed and wallpaper experience.
- HD image option and a clean, dark, Material 3 interface.
- No account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Mohakash is open source. Pictures and explanations belong to their creators and are shown with the credit supplied by NASA APOD. This is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA.
