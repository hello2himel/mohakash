- New optional Carousel mode: rotate the live wallpaper through valid APOD photos from today’s date in past years.
- Carousel interval is calculated from the number of verified photos; unavailable or broken images are skipped.
- Choose “More pictures a day” during onboarding or change it later in Settings.
- New APOD notifications toggle: notify only for a newly installed regular APOD, never for carousel rotations.
- Background APOD and carousel work now waits for connectivity and retries transient failures automatically.
