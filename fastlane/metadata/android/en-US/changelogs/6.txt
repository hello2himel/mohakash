A refreshed Android interface built around one full-screen picture feed.

- Today and Archive are now one feed: swipe between older and newer pictures.
- Search the feed, filter by year and media type, or jump to a date.
- Saved is now a dedicated tab, with your pictures available offline.
- Feed images follow the app's Fill or Fit image scaling setting.
- The full entry view shows the whole picture, uncropped, with the description below.
