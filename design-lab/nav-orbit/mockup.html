Orbit Line

A three-station instrument for the sky. The dock keeps the rocket at the centre, but moves its weight from a giant disc to a position on a single orbital track. No protrusion, no accent slab, labels back under every icon — and the bar stays quiet under a photograph.

Baselines

Every label box starts at 58 dp from the dock top. The side icons sit at 26–50 dp, so the track crosses the icon band at ≈37–38 dp; the node centres at 30 dp.

System inset

The bar background runs behind the navigationBars inset; the 80 dp content is never shifted into it. Gesture 24, three-button 48.

03 / States & edges

Quiet at rest, honest under stress

Selection moves the underline and the label weight; the only looping motion is the ring while a random picture is being picked. Large text grows the dock instead of clipping the labels.

The one idea

The rocket is not a button sitting on top of a bar; it is a station on the bar’s orbit. The track gives the two quiet destinations a common spatial frame and gives the random action a position rather than a size.

Why it reads

The eye gets one accent object (the ringed node), one shared baseline (58 dp), and one continuous line. The old 80 dp disc and 62 dp accent slab are gone; the 48 dp ring is smaller than a fingertip but framed by the whole 104 dp cell.
